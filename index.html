📱 Five years in the making · Heart Hax in bloom

From Termux Girl to Heart Hax 💜

For five years, I was a Termux girl, building custom terminals on my phone and dreaming of Linux.

Then one day, that dream was placed in my hands in computer form.

What looked like a little machine to the world felt like a doorway to me, a chance, a future, and a dream finally becoming real.

With a brother beside me, Heart Hax began to bloom. 👻💚

And from those little beginnings came learning Linux, dual boot, creating tools, building terminals, designing websites, and discovering things I never imagined I could do.

I look at our creations today and still can't believe how far this little dream has come. And the most beautiful part? I didn't have to walk that journey alone. 💗

🐺 💗 💜 Forever in my heart

To My Brothers I Hold Dear to My Heart

To the brothers who have sat beside me, shared their knowledge, helped me learn, encouraged my dreams, and kept me afloat when others tried to hold a girl down...

No matter where life takes us, I'll never forget what you've done for me or what these years have meant.
